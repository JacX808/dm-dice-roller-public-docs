# DM Dice Roller Privacy Policy

Last updated: Oct 24, 2026

## Summary

DM Dice Roller has no accounts, and your rolls and settings are never sent to us. Everything the app saves stays on your phone. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some device information to show them, as described below.

This policy covers the DM Dice Roller app for Android, published by JacX808 ("we"). Contact: jacx808.games@gmail.com

## Information stored on your device

The app saves a few things on your phone so it opens the way you left it:

- The dice you've picked, your modifier, and advantage or disadvantage
- Your settings: dice and felt colours, sound, shake to roll, and whether the dice box is open
- Your last 50 rolls

This stays in the app's private storage on your device and is never uploaded or sent to us. **Clear history** in the app removes your rolls. Clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstalling the app, removes everything.

## Advertising

The app shows a banner ad from Google AdMob, provided by Google LLC (Google Ireland Limited for users in the UK and EEA). To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect:

- Your device's advertising ID
- Your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location
- Device and app details, such as phone model, Android version and app version
- How you interact with ads, such as views and taps

Google handles this data under its own privacy policy, as an independent controller. We don't receive it. See [how Google uses information from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Where the law requires it, including in the UK, EEA and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. Without consent, Google shows non-personalised ads.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's **Settings → Google → Ads**. You can also manage ad personalisation at [My Ad Center](https://myadcenter.google.com).

## Device permissions

- **Internet and network access:** used to load ads. Rolling dice works offline.
- **Vibration:** a short tick when you roll and when the dice land.
- **Motion sensor:** used only if you turn on Shake to roll. Movement is checked on your phone to spot a shake, and is never stored or sent anywhere.

## Sharing and selling

We don't collect, sell or share your personal data. The only data that leaves your device is what Google collects to show ads, described above.

## Children

DM Dice Roller is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them. If you have concerns about a child using the app, contact us.

## Your rights and security

Under UK data protection law (UK GDPR) you can ask what personal data is held about you, and ask for it to be corrected or deleted. We hold no personal data about you: your app data stays on your device, and you can delete it at any time.

For data Google collects to show ads, use Google's controls at [My Ad Center](https://myadcenter.google.com) or contact Google directly. You can also complain to the [Information Commissioner's Office](https://ico.org.uk).

App data is kept in Android's private app storage, which other apps can't read.
